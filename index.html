02 About the farm

A farm you can name, not a WhatsApp number.

For three years our customers ordered by sending a message to a personal phone number, hoping someone would reply with a price. Produce changed weekly, prices were never written down, and new customers had no way to verify who we were. That is exactly the trust gap this website closes.

Every crop listed here is grown on our four acres in Faridabad or sourced from two neighbouring certified farms. We list the harvest date, the price per kilo, and our organic certificate number on every product page. Orders placed through the site are confirmed by email, not by chat.
